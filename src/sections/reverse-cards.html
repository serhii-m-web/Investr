The Current Reality is Broken

{{{picture "/images/reverse-cards-1.png" alt="Founder reviewing investor outreach on a laptop" width="625" height="508" class="reverse-cards__item-image"}}}

For Founders

You have 6 months of runway, a product that works, and customers who love it. But your pitch deck has been sent to 200 investors and you've heard back from 4. The ones who replied wanted a warm intro you don't have. The ones you met through accelerators invested in something else. The platform where investors actually live doesn't look anything like how you actually pitch.

{{{picture "/images/reverse-cards-2.png" alt="Investor sorting through a crowded inbox" width="625" height="508" class="reverse-cards__item-image"}}}

For Investors

Your inbox is a deal flow cemetery. 40 cold emails a week from founders who found you on Crunchbase. None of them are at the stage you invest in. None of them are in the sectors you care about. The deals you actually wanted to see came through three layers of warm intro that took 6 weeks to arrange. By then, someone else had already written the check.
