What Makes Investr Different

Mobile-first,
Not Mobile-adapted

Investr was designed for mobile from day one. The entire experience — from pitch discovery to closing a deal — works perfectly on a phone. Because that's where business actually happens now.

{{{picture "/images/different-1.png" alt="Mobile-first Investr interface" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}

Video Pitches
Change the Game

Investors back people before they back businesses. A video pitch communicates conviction, clarity, and personality in 90 seconds. Text decks can't do that. Investr makes video the default, not an add-on.

{{{picture "/images/different-2.png" alt="Founder recording a video pitch" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}

Invite-only
Network Quality

The referral system keeps investor quality high. Founders pitch to people actively deploying capital at the stages and sectors they care about.

{{{picture "/images/different-3.png" alt="Invite-only investor network" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}

Stage-adaptive
Information

Pitch cards adapt to the founder's business stage. An Idea-stage founder isn't forced to fill in revenue metrics they don't have. A Series A founder can upload full P&L documents.

{{{picture "/images/different-4.png" alt="Stage-adaptive pitch card" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}

Real-time
Founder Analytics

Investr shows founders exactly what's happening: who viewed, how long they watched, who saved, who messaged. Data that makes every iteration smarter.

{{{picture "/images/different-5.png" alt="Founder analytics dashboard" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}

Direct
Communication

No warm intros. No intermediaries. Investors and founders communicate directly within the platform. The deal moves at the speed of the conversation.

{{{picture "/images/different-6.png" alt="Direct messaging between founder and investor" width="300" height="300" class="different__image"}}}
